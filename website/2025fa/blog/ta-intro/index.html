Introducing the TA!

by Kei Imada August 26, 2025

Hello everyone! I’m a third year Ph.D. student studying PL and I’ll be the TA for CS 6120. I’ve taken this course in 2023 Fall, and I’m excited to help you all as you dive into compilers. I’ll mainly be active on Zulip, so feel free to reach out there with any questions about the material or assignments.

This is the course blog for CS 6120, the PhD-level compilers course in the computer science department at Cornell. You can subscribe to posts on the blog with RSS.
